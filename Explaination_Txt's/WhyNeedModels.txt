Say we have a restaurant and we need to sell 

1. Chicken Momo = 200
2. Veg Momo = 150   
3. Milk Tea = 50

So our system/application needs a consistent way to represent them.
Therefore, in C# we can create class called Product.
And this product can have properties such as ID, Name, and Price.

Note: This is not our database.
We connect this Models with the database.
