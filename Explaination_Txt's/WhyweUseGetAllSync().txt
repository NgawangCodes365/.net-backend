For that we use GetAllSync().


        public async Task<IEnumerable<Product>> GetAllAsync()
            what this means :

            1. Async: this allows method to perform asynchronous operation using await.
    Basically: the explaination is Data queries can take time. Therefore, asynchronous operations allow the app to avoid blocking 
    a thread while waiting for database response.

    2. Task<> : it means it will be a async operation.

    3. IEnumerable<Product> : It promises to return a group of product back.
